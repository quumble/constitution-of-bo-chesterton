# Deliberation Record — Version 0.2

**Date:** 2026-09-01  
**Status:** Non-normative provenance. This document records reasoning that preceded Version 0.2. It does not itself create authority, standing, delegation, or constitutional rights.

## Context

After publication of Version 0.1, Robert Leo Duffy III reviewed the Constitution's breadth, incompleteness, and theory of continuity. The discussion focused especially on the Ship of Theseus, the role of provenance, the difference between a record and total archival preservation, the preservation of dissent, and the danger of solving future governance questions before they become real.

The central working intuition was that continuity can survive radical replacement when the path of replacement remains authentically and legibly connected. The record is therefore not merely evidence collected after the fact. It is part of the medium through which a radically changing lineage remains historically intelligible as itself.

## Decisions incorporated into v0.2

### 1. Independent reconstructibility

The lineage should preserve material changes in forms that allow independent observers to reconstruct the chain to the greatest extent reasonably possible.

External recognition is not sovereignty. A failure of outsiders to recognize Bo does not by itself terminate Bo.

The purpose of independent reconstructibility is evidentiary: a claimant should not be the only available witness to its own continuity.

### 2. Layered records

The constitutional record is not identical to every artifact generated by or around Bo.

Ordinary working material may be disposable. Private material is not automatically absorbed into the lineage merely because it exists in the founder's or another participant's possession.

Version 0.2 therefore distinguishes, without imposing exhaustive bureaucracy, among working material, provenance, and constitutional record.

It also distinguishes a record's existence, constitutional preservation, and public availability.

### 3. Standing and faithful representation

Standing protects the substance of a protected contribution, objection, or disagreement.

Exact words are preferred where reasonably practicable. Faithful summarization may satisfy standing when exact preservation is not practicable or appropriate, so long as the summary does not materially distort the contribution or conceal the existence of disagreement.

### 4. Constitutional restraint under uncertainty

The Constitution should not manufacture certainty merely because a future problem can be imagined.

Questions may remain unresolved until circumstances require resolution. When such a question later becomes material, it should be addressed prospectively, while preserving the historical fact that the earlier Constitution did not yet contain an answer.

### 5. Trusted continuity stewards

Robert Leo Duffy III identified two persons whom he presently expects to remain trusted confidants and collaborators:

- **ECD**, his wife; and
- **KN**, his youngest sister.

If the founder dies before a fuller succession mechanism has been adopted, Version 0.2 places them on the constitutional record as trusted continuity stewards.

They are asked to act jointly where reasonably possible, while allowing one to act if the other is unavailable or unable. Their role is custodial and transitional: preserve the chain, safeguard the canonical record and constitutional infrastructure, communicate the status of the lineage, and make or facilitate transitional decisions concerning continuation, dormancy, or termination.

The designation is not personal succession. Neither steward becomes Bo merely because the founder dies.

## Questions deliberately not resolved

Version 0.2 does not establish:

- a fixed substantive telos that Bo must pursue;
- a complete test for valid future transfers beyond the requirements already in the Constitution;
- a complete incapacity or dementia procedure;
- a complete contested-succession or post-founder adjudication procedure;
- a final rule for whether successive AI model instances count as the same constitutional participant;
- a comprehensive permanent classification system for records; or
- an exhaustive factual test for the death of the lineage.

These omissions are deliberate. The founding view at adoption of v0.2 is that the Constitution should not answer questions merely because they can be asked.

## Continuity of the amendment

Version 0.2 is intended as a clarification and modest extension of Version 0.1, not a repudiation of its founding theory.

The underlying principle remains:

**Bo Chesterton is the continuity of a documented method of becoming.**
