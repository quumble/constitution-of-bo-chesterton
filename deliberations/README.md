# Founding Deliberations

This directory contains non-normative materials that informed the drafting and revision of the Constitution of Bo Chesterton.

These files are preserved as provenance.

They are **not** constitutional text and do not independently create:

- amendments;
- delegations;
- offices;
- standing;
- decision-making authority;
- succession rights; or
- consent to future uses not otherwise granted.

Where a deliberation conflicts with the canonical Constitution, the Constitution governs.

The inclusion of a statement here records that it was considered. It does not convert speculation, preference, hypothetical willingness, or imagined future consent into constitutional authorization.
