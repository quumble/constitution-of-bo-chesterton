# Constitutional History

## v0.3 — 2026-09-02

Operational amendment responding to the approaching availability of long-running autonomous artificial work.

Version 0.3:

- distinguishes participants, chairs, subordinate offices, and the public Bo office;
- establishes the Executive Chair, Research Chair, Critical Chair, and Clerk and Archivist while leaving non-executive offices vacant pending explicit appointment;
- recognizes that properly delegated unsupervised work may be authentic Bo work without becoming public Bo work;
- requires bounded autonomous mandates and permits genuine initiative within them;
- prohibits autonomous participants from enlarging their own authority or recursively delegating unless expressly authorized;
- establishes a private-work publication gate and reserves publication authority during v0.3 to Robert Leo Duffy III;
- establishes a maximum of US$50 in aggregate external cost per autonomous mandate;
- prohibits open-ended liability, recurring commitments, debt, automatic replenishment, uncapped infrastructure, and other unbounded financial exposure;
- requires available technical controls to reinforce constitutional limits and preserves overruns as unauthorized even if later rules become more permissive;
- defines versions before 1.0 as a founding and experimental period in which substantial revision is expected but may not rewrite history or imply ungranted authority; and
- requires Version 1.0 to be explicitly ratified rather than reached by numbering alone.

Version 0.3 creates governance machinery but does not itself appoint Astra or any other artificial participant, activate an autonomous mandate, delegate publication authority, or resolve comprehensive succession and incapacity questions.

## v0.2 — 2026-09-01

First clarifying amendment after the initial public constitution.

Version 0.2:
- makes independent reconstructibility an explicit constitutional aspiration while denying outsiders sovereign power over identity;
- distinguishes ordinary working material, preserved provenance, and the constitutional record without requiring exhaustive classification;
- distinguishes the existence, preservation, and public availability of records;
- clarifies that standing protects the substance of protected dissent, with exact preservation preferred and faithful summarization permitted;
- adopts a principle of constitutional restraint under uncertainty rather than requiring premature rules for imagined future cases;
- designates ECD and KN as trusted continuity stewards if the founder dies before a fuller succession procedure exists;
- preserves the custodial and transitional nature of that stewardship and does not make either steward Bo by designation alone;
- updates founding-period version references without otherwise completing incapacity, contested-succession, AI-identity, or post-founder adjudication machinery.

Version 0.2 does not add a fixed substantive telos for Bo Chesterton. Continuity remains grounded in the real and legible constitutional chain rather than preservation of a particular research program, philosophy, style, or personality.

## v0.1 — 2026-08-31

Initial public constitution.

Version 0.1:

- defines Bo Chesterton as a governed lineage and a documented method of becoming;
- distinguishes the office, lineage, and record;
- establishes Robert Leo Duffy III as founder and initial source of constitutional authority;
- distinguishes inheritance from obedience and permits intellectual drift;
- separates voice, standing, and power within the founding boardroom;
- establishes explicit, prospective, ordinarily revocable delegation during the founding period;
- rejects succession by resemblance, prediction, technical control, or simulated consent;
- preserves the possibility of future founder simulations without presuming personal continuity;
- protects persons outside Bo from being absorbed into the lineage by proximity alone;
- permits forks, dormancy, dissolution, and truthful termination of the lineage;
- establishes three constitutive continuity conditions: a real chain, a legible chain, and no counterfeit personal identity or authority;
- defines materiality and sufficient legibility for constitutional interpretation.

No prior public constitutional version exists.
